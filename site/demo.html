How to read ForgeRank

Three checks before you trust a rank.

ForgeRank is designed to expose the boundaries that ordinary leaderboards hide.

01

Check the cohort.

“#1” means first among the repositories included in the named observed set—not first on GitHub.

02

Check the clock.

Totals and trends can have different observation times. Stale evidence remains visibly stale.

03

Open the reasons.

The score version, raw inputs, missing dimensions, and confidence multiplier remain inspectable.
